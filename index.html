FAQ

Questions clients usually ask first

How much do your services cost?

The first 30-minute consultation is free. After that, you receive a written quote based on the scope of your request, and nothing starts until you approve it.

Do we sign a contract?

Yes. Before work begins we sign a simple service agreement in English that sets out the scope, timeline and fees, so both sides know exactly what to expect.

How do I pay?

Payment is by bank transfer, from overseas or from a Vietnamese account. The payment schedule is written into the service agreement.

Can you help me before I arrive in Vietnam?

Yes. Many clients start while they are still abroad. I can search, inspect, communicate, negotiate and coordinate locally on your behalf so things are ready when you arrive.

What if I don't know which service I need?

You don't have to choose. Tell me where you are, what you're trying to do and what you're struggling with, and I'll suggest the most suitable way forward.

How do we communicate?

In English or Vietnamese, by WhatsApp, Viber, Zalo, email or video call, whichever is easiest for you.
